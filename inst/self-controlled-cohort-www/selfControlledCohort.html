Self Controlled Cohort Viewer

This module allows you to explore self controlled cohort results. The self controlled cohort design estimates the association between an exposure (drug) and an outcome by comparing the outcome rate while a person is exposed to the outcome rate while that same person is unexposed.

Signal discovery - shows all exposure-outcome pairs with the number of databases showing a potential benefit or risk (using the calibrated estimates) and the meta analytic (evidence synthesis) estimate. Use the filters to restrict the pairs shown.

Pair explorer - select a pair in the signal discovery view to see the detailed results per database and the meta analysis, a forest plot, a systematic error plot of the negative control estimates, and boxplots of the time at risk and time to outcome distributions.
